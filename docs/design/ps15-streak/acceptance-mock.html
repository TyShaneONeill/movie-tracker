Round 10 · direction D

The founder
shipped the camera.

Round 4 put three cameras up for a vote. None of them won — Tyshane drew the answer himself in a separate design session and sent back a high-fidelity handoff: baked SVG geometry per state, a state-palette table, a two-layer beam spec, and the whole screen re-specified at 393pt. Round 5 was a transplant, not a reinterpretation. Colours, geometry, typography and spacing below are his, copied across value for value.

What changed in round 10

  • The warm wash is locked in, and it stops leaking. He picked 2B out of round 9’s two grounds, so the plain card and the 2A / 2B sub-toggle are gone — the wash is just what light mode does under a lit hero. It was also bleeding: round 9 let it overshoot the hero by 26px at each end to avoid a seam, which put warm tint on the tabs pill above it (visible on FRIENDS · SOON) and on the message card below. It is now bounded by the hero and masked to zero before it reaches those bounds, so there is nothing to clip and no edge to see. Outside the hero band the screen is pixel-for-pixel the plain ground.
  • Round 9 — the screening room is deleted; light mode gets the founder’s warm light on white. Round 7’s reasoning was that projection needs darkness, so it handed the hero a dark band to sit in. He answered with a browser mock proving the opposite: the beam on white was too weak, not too pale. Repainted at ~60% alpha at the lens and falling off linearly to the far edge, the cone reads as light on paper. The dark band, its vignette, its top highlight and its forced dark token block are all gone.
  • Round 9 — every light-mode value is sampled off his screenshot. Idle numeral zinc, lit numeral near-black #18181b under a 16px warm glow instead of round 6’s 90px smear, milestone numeral an amber-brown gradient with a #b45309 chip, and the idle camera moved to the light half of the zinc ramp. His lit and milestone camera palettes decode back to the handoff hexes exactly, so those are untouched.
  • Round 8 — the milestone chip stops hiding under the camera. On screens narrower than the 393pt artboard the sub row kept its full width and slid the gold MILESTONE chip onto the barrel, where gold on gold made it vanish outright; a bug since the round-5 transplant, in both themes. The number block now carries a container-query ceiling — everything left of the lens, less 20px of air — and the sub row wraps, so below ~370pt the chip drops to its own line instead of into the camera. At 393 the hero renders identically to round 7, element for element.

The handoff is the acceptance spec now. Not this page’s taste — his measurements. The build gets compared against these mocks with device screenshots, side by side, at the same states and both themes, and it isn’t done until they match. Where this page deviates from design_handoff_streak_camera/README.md it says so out loud; everywhere else, a diff is a bug.

D1

The cell that unlocksFree · not Plus-gated

Not a new card dropped into Stats — the existing Watch Streaks cell, with the padlock taken off. Same grid, same dividers, same badge row. Siblings dimmed so you can see what changed.

Going deeper
your insights
  • Three things change in that cell, and nothing else on the screen moves. The padlock becomes a chevron, the three-line description becomes a number plus one line, and the grey clock-and-Coming soon badge becomes the green star-and-Live for you the other cells already carry. Row heights, dividers, and badge baselines stay exactly where they are.
  • No glyph in this cell — the number is the status. Rose with a soft halo when today is already in the can, gold at a milestone, flat grey when today's frame is still blank. One element changes, and it's the one your eye lands on anyway; the camera stays a destination-screen thing, so this cell never competes with the three beside it.
  • Measured, not eyeballed. Section inset 18, cell padding 13, titles Outfit ExtraBold 15.5/21, descriptions 12.5/17.5, badges 11.5 semibold pinned to the bottom of the row, hairline grid at the cell edges. These numbers came off the shipped screen and are the pass/fail criteria.
  • Free, deliberately. A streak only Plus members can see can't drive the habit that sells Plus. The cell and the screen both ship ungated; the Friends tab is where paid can live later.
D2

The streak screen

The handoff, rebuilt on a 393pt artboard and wired to four states. The camera, the beam, the chrome and the copy are the founder's; two additions are marked where they appear.

  • The camera is transplanted, not reinterpreted. Every coordinate is the shipped asset: body 56,62 · 136×84 r24, barrel 20,86 · 42×30, lens face 6,80 · 18×42, optical axis at y=100. All three palettes are the README's role table verbatim, down to the near-black film discs that stop the active state reading as one flat red and the light-gold vent panel that makes the milestone body glow from inside.
  • The light source is hidden, which is the whole trick. Part 13 is a blurred halo, a white core and a horizontal flare sitting on the lens axis just inside the glass. The beam is two clip-path layers behind the camera and under the number — a warm cone plus a hot white core — and its narrow end is centred on that axis with the right edge tucked five pixels under the barrel. You never see where the light starts, so it reads as projected rather than painted.
  • The calendar got quieter. Round 4's rose→amber ramp is gone. Runs are now one flat muted pill, rgba(225,29,72,.13) with #fecdd3 ink, so the month reads as texture rather than a heat map — and the only saturated thing on it is the milestone day, a gold disc with a three-layer ring that punches a hole through the run it ends.
  • KEEP — the extend pills. Not in the handoff; kept because the routing has to live somewhere. They render only while today is blank, under the message card, three equal columns, each labelled with its destination in mono — SCAN, HOME, FEED. Once you've logged, the message card stands alone and the screen goes quiet.
  • KEEP — the rain cloud. Also not in the handoff. A covered day keeps its pill and swaps the numeral for a small gold cloud, which is the honest picture: the run didn't break, the weather covered it. Everything else gold on this screen is spent on the milestone — the disc, the ring, the number gradient and the mono chip.

Friends is deliberately dead. The tab renders, is visibly disabled, and carries a mono SOON tag. It reserves the space and sets the expectation without shipping a social graph we don't have yet.

Deep links
Scan ticket → the ticket-scan module on the Scan tab. First Take → the First Take composer on Home. Comment → the Feed tab. Rate a film (day-one state) → Home. All four dismiss the streak screen on navigate rather than stacking on top of it, so backing out of the composer lands you on the tab you were sent to, not back here.
Data depth
The calendar can only paint what the API returns, which is a 14-day activity window. Days older than that render plain, not empty — see the Day 30 · milestone state, where a 30-day run shows only 14 painted days and Aug 1 stays blank. A month-deep calendar needs a backend constant change (ACTIVITY_WINDOW_DAYS) plus an index check; the in-screen mono caption tells the truth until then.
Later
Days where a ticket was scanned get a 45°-tilted ticket icon behind the numeral — a second layer of texture on the calendar that distinguishes "you rated something" from "you were actually in a cinema." Needs the per-day action type on the calendar payload, so it's v3 at the earliest.
Later
A quick-link to your own streak on your profile — a single line or chip that opens this screen, visible only to you. Explicitly never rendered on anyone else's view of your profile: the streak is a private habit metric, not a public score, and the moment it's visible to others it becomes something to perform. Cheap to build, but it needs the "is this me" check wired before it ships, so it isn't a v1 line item.
Later
A separate concept: friends' weekly progress, as its own surface rather than a tab on this screen. Not a leaderboard and not a streak comparison — a week-shaped view of what the people you follow have been watching. It shares the Friends slot reserved here, but it's a different product decision and wants its own round.
Light mode
Light mode reads the light as saturated warm colour on white. The round-7 screening room is gone. The founder redrew the hero in his own browser and the answer was that the beam on a white card was never too pale, it was too weak: painted at ~60% alpha at the lens and falling off in a straight line to the far edge, the same cone reads as light lying on paper rather than dirt on it. So the hero is back on the light ground, and the four state values are his — idle numeral zinc #a1a1aa, lit numeral near-black #18181b under a 16px warm glow, milestone numeral an amber-brown gradient #c26208 → #e9910c with a #b45309 chip, and the idle camera the light half of the zinc ramp instead of the handoff's dark silhouette. Lit and milestone camera palettes are untouched — sampled back out of his Display-P3 screenshot they return the handoff hexes exactly. The ground is settled: the warm wash stays. He picked it out of round 9’s two, so it is no longer a variant — a lit hero sits in a barely-there warm radial, an unlit one on plain white, and the plain-ground alternative and its toggle are deleted. The wash is bounded by the hero and masked to nothing before it reaches the hero’s top and bottom edges, so it never tints the tabs pill above it or the message card below — measured at zero difference from the plain ground everywhere outside the hero band. Dark mode is untouched — every rule is scoped to the light theme, and dark renders pixel-identical to round 8 in all four states at 393pt and 360pt.
The camera
Source of truth is design_handoff_streak_camera/ — the three baked SVGs plus the README's palette table. In the app this is react-native-svg with one palette object per state instead of CSS vars, blur via feGaussianBlur, and the beam as two absolutely-positioned gradient views behind the number. The whole-camera drop-shadow can be dropped on Android if it costs anything, and the milestone gradient numeral falls back to solid #fde68a if MaskedView is more trouble than it's worth.
Deviations
Three, all deliberate. The extend pills and the rain cloud are ours, kept at the founder's word and marked in the notes above. The footnote says “last 14 days fetched” rather than the handoff's “LAST 30 DAYS”, because the API window really is 14 days and the type should not lie about it — same size, same tracking, honest text. Everything else on this screen is the handoff.

Archive · round 4

The three cameras that were up for a vote

All three were retired by the founder’s handoff camera before anyone pressed one. Kept as one line each, for the record.

  • Bold outline. Theme red, one chunky stroke weight, flat fill, no interior detail — the Duolingo move. Was the stated lean and the mock’s default.
  • Line art. A single 2.2px weight in the text colour, nothing filled; it lit by turning rose rather than gaining colour. Letterboxd-quiet.
  • Low poly. No outline anywhere — three tones of one hue cut into flat facets, depth implied by which plane caught the light.
